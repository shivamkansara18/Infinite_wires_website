Anatomy of the wire

One conductor. Nothing else inside.

This is winding wire, not a cable. Every length carries a single solid copper conductor under a white outer coating. No strands and no multiple cores.

    White outer coating

    Applied to the overall diameter you specify, evenly all the way round.

    Our speciality at every size. The wire moulds easily by hand.

About us

Making winding wire since 2008.

Infinite Wires and Components Pvt. Ltd. manufactures submersible copper winding wire for pump motors of all sizes.

We began in 2008 with one product and one aim: winding wire that makes the winder's work easier. Since then our skilled team has refined a single thing above all, a finish that stays soft and smooth at any size.

We use only 100% pure EC grade copper from Birla Sterlite. Every length has one solid copper conductor and a white outer coating, made to the copper diameter and overall diameter you specify.

Because the wire is soft, it is easy to mould. Labour on the winding line can lay it into pump motors quickly and cleanly, which is why pump makers and rewinders come back to us.
